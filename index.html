    Honest boundary

    A web page cannot type into other apps. The receiver copies text to your clipboard; you paste with Ctrl/Cmd + V. On-device speech availability depends on Chrome/Android and installed language packs. Quiet Bridge does not silently fall back to cloud transcription.
